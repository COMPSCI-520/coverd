# Coverd: UMass Shift Management System

## 📖 Project Overview

Currently, the UMass dining commons heavily rely on manual Google Sheets to manage the complex schedules of student workers. Many of these students are international and work variable shifts of 3 to 8 hours multiple times a week. This ad-hoc approach presents several critical issues:

* **Privacy Concerns:** Personal schedules and availability are exposed to the entire staff without restriction.
* **Disorganized Communication:** The process for dropping a shift and requesting a replacement relies on scattered channels like group texts, making it incredibly difficult for managers to track and approve changes efficiently.
* **Compliance Risks:** It creates a massive compliance risk for international students who must strictly adhere to a 20-hour weekly legal limit. Without an automated safeguard, it is alarmingly easy to accidentally exceed these hours when picking up a desperate peer's dropped shift.

To resolve these systemic inefficiencies, our team proposes **Coverd**, a centralized Shift Management System. The project involves specifying, designing, implementing, and evaluating a software application with a frontend and backend to streamline this entire process.

The application features a dedicated student portal where workers can seamlessly view their current schedules, formally request to drop specific shifts, and browse a centralized marketplace to pick up available shifts dropped by their peers. To maintain operational oversight, a separate manager dashboard allows administrators to easily review, approve, or deny these shift drops and swaps from a single interface.
